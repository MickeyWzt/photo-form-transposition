# Quality gate

Inspect the generated image at normal size and as a small thumbnail. Do not judge only by attractiveness.

## Blocking failures

Reject the result if any condition is true:

- it is mainly a filter, full redraw, hard cutout, or ordinary double exposure;
- the target needs a caption or explanation to be recognized;
- completing the target required prominent invented anatomy or decorative structure;
- the photograph survives only as flat fill and has lost its directional flow or depth;
- the subject is isolated from the field by a uniformly closed edge;
- clutter, fragmentation, or effects overpower the two main readings;
- unrelated visual content appears.

## Score the result

Score each dimension from 0 to 2:

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Target recognition | unreadable | delayed or unstable | clear at intended strength |
| Source identity | lost | partly perceptible | distinctly preserved |
| Structural derivation | mostly invented | mixed | anchors arise from source |
| Environment continuity | isolated | one weak bridge | two or three credible bridges |
| Boundary quality | closed or arbitrary | partly selective | open, controlled, directional |
| Restraint | decorative or busy | minor excess | clean and necessary |

Pass when the total is at least 10/12 and no blocking failure applies. Treat 8–9 as revision territory. Below 8, reconsider the target rather than polishing the same composition.

## Diagnose before revising

- **Unreadable target:** strengthen two recognition anchors, not the entire contour.
- **Closed-mask result:** open one major edge and add two directional environment bridges.
- **Source identity lost:** restore one dominant photographic carrier and reduce invented structure.
- **Illustrated result:** remove outlines, veins, feather marks, or anatomy not carried by the source.
- **Weak fusion:** connect through source atmosphere or texture; do not apply a uniform opacity fade.
- **Busy result:** retain two to four carriers, remove secondary fragments, and drop the supporting effect.
- **Forced target:** return to candidate selection and choose a lower-invention form.

Make one targeted revision, repeat the quality gate, and report any remaining trade-off honestly.
