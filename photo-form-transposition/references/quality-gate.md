# Quality gate

Inspect normal and thumbnail views. Do not judge only by attractiveness.

## Blocking failures

Reject the result if any condition is true:

- it is mainly a filter, full redraw, generic photo mask, or ordinary double exposure;
- the target has no credible keystone carrier;
- the role map is absent and the photograph functions only as decorative fill;
- recognition depends mainly on invented limbs, joints, facial parts, veins, outlines, or symmetry;
- source direction, depth, or material identity is lost;
- clutter, fragmentation, or effects overpower the two readings;
- unrelated visual content appears.

## Shared score

Score each dimension from 0 to 2:

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Target recognition | unreadable | unstable | clear at intended strength |
| Source identity | lost | partial | distinctly preserved |
| Keystone strength | absent | plausible | decisive and visible |
| Role mapping | generic fill | mixed | three or more distinct roles |
| Completion economy | invented anatomy dominates | minor excess | cues are sparse and source-derived |
| Restraint | decorative or busy | minor excess | clean and necessary |

Pass the shared score at 10/12 or higher with no blocking failure. Treat 8–9 as one-revision territory. Below 8, reconsider the target or architecture.

## Architecture gate

Apply only the selected architecture's gate.

### Contained

Pass when at least three source regions perform distinct roles, internal space remains photographically coherent, and the contour does not feel prefabricated. Do not fail a contained result merely because most of its perimeter is complete.

### Permeable

Pass when the original field or surface remains continuous and source material crosses at least two target boundaries. Selected anchors must remain legible; uniform transparency is a failure.

### Gestural

Pass when one source motion dominates the result, three or four anchors imply the target, and distal zones remain incomplete. Fail when several limbs, joints, or facial features become independently finished.

## Diagnose before revising

- **Weak keystone:** choose another target or promote a stronger source axis, path, mass, or flow.
- **Generic fill:** restore explicit carrier roles and coherent internal depth.
- **Unreadable target:** strengthen two anchors, not the entire contour.
- **Contained result looks pasted:** add one or two small texture escapes or remove unsupported edge precision.
- **Permeable result is isolated:** add two directional crossings from the original field.
- **Gestural result is anatomical:** dissolve completed parts and restore the dominant source motion.
- **Completion budget exceeded:** remove eyes, paws, joints, vein diagrams, decorative outlines, or repeated detail.
- **Busy result:** keep two to four carriers and drop the supporting effect.

Make one targeted revision, repeat the shared and architecture gates, and report any remaining trade-off honestly.
